Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our admission process

Admission FAQs

Q: When does the academic year start?
A: The academic year begins in April.

Q: Which board does the school follow?
A: The school is affiliated with RBSE (Rajasthan Board of Secondary Education).

Q: Which mediums are available?
A: English Medium is available from Playgroup to Class 12. Hindi Medium is available from Class 6 to Class 12.

Q: What documents are required for admission?
A: Birth certificate, previous school records, passport-size photos, and address proof.

Q: Is transportation available?
A: Yes, we provide bus services covering major areas around Sanchore.

Q: Do you offer scholarships?
A: Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships. Contact us at +91 925 615 1729 for details.
